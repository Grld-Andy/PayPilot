# 🔥 PayPilot – The Subscription Manager for Small Businesses  

## 1️⃣ Focus on Small Businesses, Not Just SaaS  
✅ Target **gyms, freelancers, and service-based businesses** instead of just SaaS startups.  
✅ Make it easy for **fitness centers, salons, and coaches** to manage subscriptions.  

## 2️⃣ Simple, No-Code Setup 🚀  
✅ **No coding required** – Business owners can create subscription plans in minutes.  
✅ **Pre-built templates** for common subscription businesses (**Gym, Salon, Freelancers, Coaching, Rentals**).  

## 3️⃣ More than Just Billing – A Full Subscription Dashboard 📊  
✅ **Revenue & customer insights** → Businesses see growth trends without needing an external analytics tool.  
✅ **Automated notifications** (failed payments, renewals, expiring cards) via **email/SMS**.  

## 4️⃣ Built-in Chat & Customer Engagement 🗨️  
✅ **Real-time WebSocket chat** → Businesses can engage directly with their subscribers.  
✅ **Upsell & Discounts** → Offer special pricing to retain users before they cancel.  

## 5️⃣ Multi-Payment Support (Beyond Stripe) 💳  
✅ **Stripe + PayPal + Crypto payments** → Offer more ways to pay.  
✅ Businesses can accept **Apple Pay, Google Pay, and mobile money** (for African/Asian markets).  

## 6️⃣ White-Label Option for Businesses 🎨  
✅ Businesses can **custom-brand their subscription portal**.  
✅ Allow **custom domain support** (e.g., `payments.mygym.com`).

✅ **For small businesses & gyms**, not just SaaS startups.
