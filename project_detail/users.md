# 🛠️ User Roles & Their Access  

| **User Type**             | **Who Are They?**                                      | **What Can They Do?** |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Business Owners (Admins)**  | Gym owners, freelancers, coaches, subscription-based services.  | ✅ Create & manage subscription plans <br> ✅ Track payments & revenue analytics <br> ✅ Send invoices & automate reminders <br> ✅ Manage customers & team members |
| **Clients (Subscribers)** | Gym members, customers, students, coaching clients.   | ✅ Subscribe, upgrade, or cancel plans <br> ✅ View payment history & invoices <br> ✅ Receive reminders for renewals <br> ✅ Update payment details |
| **Staff / Assistants**    | Gym managers, accountants, customer support staff.    | ✅ View subscription reports & analytics <br> ✅ Manage customer issues <br> ❌ Cannot access business financials |
| **Super Admin (Platform Owner - Us)** | The person managing PayPilot itself. | ✅ Manage all businesses using the platform <br> ✅ Handle Stripe payouts & transactions <br> ✅ View platform-wide revenue reports |
| **Affiliates / Resellers (Optional)** | People who refer businesses to PayPilot. | ✅ Track referred users <br> ✅ Earn commissions per new business signup |

# 🎯 Target Businesses  

## 1️⃣ Gyms & Fitness Studios 🏋️  
✅ Manage monthly memberships & class packages  
✅ Automate renewals & failed payment reminders  
✅ Offer discounts for long-term members  

**Example Users:**  
✔️ Local gym owners  
✔️ Personal trainers with subscription coaching  
✔️ Yoga/Pilates instructors with recurring classes  

---

## 2️⃣ Freelancers & Creators 🎨  
✅ Sell recurring design, marketing, or writing services

✔️ Web designers & developers  
✔️ Social media managers & marketers
